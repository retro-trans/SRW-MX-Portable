### Changes since v0.4.33

- Fixed the PSP rename editor's overlapping Name and Reading fields, including
  safe loading of the old overlong Cerberus default. The editable default is
  **Cerberus Ignite**, fitting the native save slot. The database's separate
  Cerberus Ignited G/S names remain unchanged. Save structure is preserved.
- Ported PSP Chapter 28's opening, battle events and aftermath to PS2, alongside
  the existing PSP stages 29–30. Retained PS2 terrain and unit balance.
- Revised the two reported Chapter 28 Ritsu lines on both platforms.
- Translated the separate native **Assist Attack**, **Simul. Attack** and
  **God Shadow** battle-animation banners on both platforms.
- Realigned equipped-part names on PSP and rewrapped all 37 part descriptions
  for each platform's panel, preserving complete effects and numeric values.
- Retained the earlier Parts-menu crash fix, restored English battle quotes,
  campaign proofreading, uniform chapter cards and interface corrections.
- Both native title screens display **v0.4.38** and **github.com/retro-trans**.
  PSP retains outlined text at the bottom right with no dark panel.

### Verification and limits

Automated checks read the current story, battle captions, chapter artwork and
native title footers from the actual output images. Native execution checks
cover PSP rename editing and Parts regressions, and PS2 scenario loading,
deployment, campaign progression, saves, fonts and title guards. All four
patches are decoded and checked against the complete target image hashes
with Retro Trans Tools. Detailed evidence is in `BUILD-TESTS-v0.4.38.json`.

Fresh in-game visual checks, a complete Chapter 28 battle clear, a full
playthrough and physical-console testing remain pending. Some bitmap labels
and the Japanese name-entry keyboard remain untranslated. The PS2 extra-scenario
port requires a new game; existing saves retain their earlier scenario layout
and save migration has not been added.

Report issues with platform, version, stage, a screenshot and a save when possible.
